## Course Arc

This course treats several apparently different subjects as instances of the same problem template:

- a decision maker must choose from a set of alternatives
- some relevant variable is unknown at decision time
- the decision may have different consequences in different scenarios
- the available information changes the structure and difficulty of the problem
- risk appears because outcomes vary across possible scenarios

The goal is to understand how decisions, uncertainty, information, and dynamics interact.

## Core Themes

| Theme | Question |
|---|---|
| Decisions | What action, variable, or policy should be chosen? |
| Uncertainty | What is unknown when the decision is made? |
| Risk | How should downside outcomes and variable consequences be evaluated? |
| Information | Who knows what, when is it known, and how is it revealed or transmitted? |
| Dynamics | How do current decisions affect future states and observations? |

---

## Key Takeaway

Stochastic control, communication, team theory, and economic decision problems are not isolated topics. They can be studied as variations of a single framework: choosing actions under incomplete information, while accounting for risk, information flow, and dynamics.
