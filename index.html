Hey there! I’m Sakshi 👋🏻, a Master’s student at IIT Bombay, working on problems at the intersection of machine learning and human preferences. My current obsessions? Building multi-expert recommender systems that truly understand what people want, exploring the math of preference optimization, and thinking about how bandits can make sequential decisions smarter.

What ties these threads together is a genuine curiosity about the gap between what people say they want and what they actually engage with — and building systems that get better at navigating that gap over time.

I live for that spark of learning something new every day.
Working on something cool in AI/ML (or just want to geek out over books or food)? Reach out — I’d love to connect!
